GDPR Notice

Information for individuals whose personal data is processed under the GDPR.

Scope and purposes

This notice covers this website and enquiries you choose to send to Aitia Media. Processing related to a separate client campaign may be governed by a campaign-specific notice. Hosting involves technical request data. Enquiries may include names, business contact details and message content.

Legal basis

Where the GDPR applies, the relevant basis depends on the activity. Answering a request for steps before entering a contract may be necessary for that request. Site delivery and security may rely on legitimate interests, subject to the rights of visitors. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it for future processing.

Access and choices

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your information. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests and to use of your data for direct marketing. You may also complain to a supervisory authority.

Recipients and retention

Hosting services handle technical requests. If you send us a LinkedIn message, the platform processes that correspondence. The brief builder keeps your entries in the current page. For a sent enquiry, ask us about its recipients, retention and any international transfers relevant to your case.

Make a request

Identify the enquiry or processing activity and the right you want to exercise. Contact Aitia Media on LinkedIn or email outreach@aitiamedia.com. Read the European Commission guidance on individual rights.
