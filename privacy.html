Of this website.

How this version of the Aitia Media website handles your information.

Project brief

The contact form prepares a project brief in your browser. It does not send the entered information to Aitia Media or save it to a database. You can copy the brief and choose to share it through LinkedIn.

Theme preference

This website uses local storage on your device to remember your selected light, dark, or system colour theme. It does not store your project brief in local storage.

External services

Links to LinkedIn, Google Drive, and project websites open external services. Their own privacy practices apply when you visit them. Fonts and the Aitia logo are served with this website. No analytics or advertising scripts are included in this website's source.

Hosting

Your hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to deliver and secure the website.

Contact

For questions about Aitia Media, connect through the Aitia Media LinkedIn page.

Images

Website and studio visuals marked as placeholders are illustrative compositions, ready to be replaced with your own imagery. They do not represent campaign results or performance data.
