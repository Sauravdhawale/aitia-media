California Privacy Notice (CCPA)

Privacy information for California residents where the CCPA, as amended by the CPRA, applies.

Website information

Browsing can generate internet activity information and online identifiers for hosting and security. Contact details, business details and correspondence reach Aitia Media if you send a message. The local brief builder prepares your text on your device.

Sources, purposes and recipients

Information comes from your browser or directly from you. The website uses it to deliver pages, remember your theme or prepare your brief. Aitia Media can use sent messages to respond. Hosting providers and the messaging platform handle data for their respective services.

California privacy rights

Eligible residents may request to know, correct or delete personal information, subject to exceptions. Rights may also include opting out of sale or sharing, limiting certain uses of sensitive information and receiving equal treatment when exercising a right.

Advertising choices

The site code does not include an advertising pixel or a sale or sharing mechanism for cross-context behavioural advertising. A Global Privacy Control signal does not require enabling any additional toggle in this interface. This statement is specific to this website.

Requests and authorised agents

Contact us with the right you wish to exercise and enough information to identify the relevant interaction. An authorised agent can identify the person they represent and their authorisation. Verification may be needed for requests involving specific records. Contact Aitia Media on LinkedIn or email outreach@aitiamedia.com. See the California Attorney General’s CCPA guidance.
