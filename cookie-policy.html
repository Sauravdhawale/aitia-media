Cookie & Storage Policy

What the site stores on your device and how to manage it.

Theme preference

The site saves your light, dark or system theme under the local storage key aitia-theme. Change it with the theme controls or remove it by clearing this site’s browser data.

Form entries and clipboard

The project brief stays in the open page. Choosing Copy brief writes the text to your clipboard. You can replace it by copying other text or using your device clipboard controls.

External services

The website source loads fonts locally and does not install advertising or analytics cookies. The hosting platform and linked services may have their own technical storage and privacy practices.
